What these numbers mean. Prediction market prices reflect what traders on Kalshi are willing to pay for a contract that pays $1 if the outcome happens. A price of $0.78 on "Democrats win the House" implies a market-priced probability of 78%. Prices are noisy, react to news, and are shaped by the traders currently active on Kalshi — they are not a forecast and they do not replace polling or election analysis. Use them as one signal among many.
