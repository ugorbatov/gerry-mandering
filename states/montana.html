Data Sources

All data comes from official, public sources. Green dots indicate live data fetched on-demand; amber indicates data updated periodically.

📋 Current representatives ● LIVE
Names, parties, photos, contact info, and term dates from @unitedstates/congress-legislators (public domain). Updated within hours of any change (deaths, resignations, special elections, party switches).
github.com/unitedstates/congress-legislators
📜 Sponsored bills ● LIVE
Bills sponsored by each rep are fetched from the official Congress.gov API when you click a district. Shows the 5 most recent sponsored bills with introduction dates and latest action.
api.congress.gov
📰 News articles ● LIVE
Recent news about each representative and state political news is fetched from NewsAPI when you click a district or state. Shows last 30 days of articles from major news outlets.
newsapi.org
💰 Campaign finance ● LIVE
Top individual donors, PACs, and employers from the FEC OpenFEC API. Click any donor or PAC name to view their complete giving history on OpenSecrets.org or FEC.gov.
api.open.fec.gov · OpenSecrets
🗳️ 2024 election results ● 2024 CERTIFIED
District-level 2024 presidential election results showing Democrat vs. Republican vote share for all 435 congressional districts. Used in district tooltips and the rep detail modal.
FEC certifications · state Secretary of State offices · cross-checked with Ballotpedia
🗺️ District boundaries ● 119TH CONGRESS
Per-state district GeoJSON loaded on demand when you click into a state. Primary source: Ben Balter's congressional-districts repo (Census TIGER data). Fallbacks: @unitedstates/districts and GovTrack.
Primary: github.com/benbalter/congressional-districts
🏛️ House seat counts ● APR 30, 2026
Current party split per state for the 119th Congress (Jan 2025 – Jan 2027). Used as fallback before live legislator data loads. Updated when major changes occur (special elections, party switches).
pressgallery.house.gov
📊 District demographics ● ACS 5-YEAR 2023
Race, ethnicity, median household income, and poverty rate for each congressional district. Source: U.S. Census Bureau American Community Survey 5-Year Estimates (2019–2023 vintage).
data.census.gov
📈 Historical House data ● 1994–2024
16 election cycles of state-by-state delegation data and national popular vote totals. Drives the time-travel slider on national and state views.
Brookings Vital Statistics · U.S. House Press Gallery
👥 State population ● VINTAGE 2025
Resident population estimates from the U.S. Census Bureau's annual Population Estimates Program (PEP). Vintage 2025 was released January 2026.
census.gov · NST-EST2025
🖼️ Member photos ● LIVE
Official portraits served from bioguide.congress.gov (primary) with fallback to theunitedstates.io's free CDN. Keyed by Bioguide ID.
bioguide.congress.gov
🗺️ Map projections (state outlines) ● TIGER/LINE 2024
State outlines drawn from the Census Bureau's TIGER/Line shapefiles via the topojson/us-atlas project.
github.com/topojson/us-atlas
Methodology notes:
• ● LIVE data is fetched on every page load — bills, news, and finance are queried when you open a district or state.
• ● Periodic data is manually updated when major changes occur (election cycles, decennial Census releases).
• Historical vote percentages use two-party share (R vs. D only) for consistent cross-cycle comparison.
• All data is non-partisan and sourced from official government records.
