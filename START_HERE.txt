                              DATA SOURCES

All demographic data comes from official U.S. government sources:

  Population Density:
    Source: U.S. Census Bureau
    Method: Land area from Geographic Data files
    Units: Persons per square mile

  Foreign-Born Population:
    Source: American Community Survey (ACS)
    Method: 5-Year Estimates (2023-2024)
    Definition: % of population born outside the United States

  U.S. Citizenship:
    Source: American Community Survey (ACS)
    Method: 5-Year Estimates (2023-2024)
    Definition: % of population that are U.S. citizens

Data is public domain and can be used freely.
