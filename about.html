Why this exists

Gerrymandering — drawing district lines to predetermine election outcomes — is the clearest way politicians rig the system. You can win elections with fewer votes. You can silence millions. And it happens in plain sight, hidden inside map shapes and census numbers.

This project turns that data into something you can see, touch, and understand.

The truth is simple: when the maps are rigged, democracy fails.

What we measure

🗺️

District Map

Every U.S. congressional district in the 119th Congress. Who represents it. Party breakdown. Election results from the FEC. Population from the Census.

⚖️

Legal Tracker

Every major redistricting lawsuit. Cases, judges, oral arguments, outcomes. AI summaries of court filings so you don't have to read the jargon.

📊

Vote Gap

The efficiency gap — how many votes it takes to win a seat vs. how many it should take. Shows where the maps are most broken.

💰

Follow the Money

Who funds redistricting fights. Donor networks, PAC spending, the people trying to rig the maps and those fighting to stop them.

How it's made

Data Collection

Pulled from public APIs and official sources. Cleaned, deduplicated, and cross-checked against multiple sources.

Visualization

Interactive maps with D3.js and MapBox. District shapes from Ben Baiter's open-source geographic data.

Lawsuit Tracking

Court dockets from Google Scholar. Summaries generated by Claude AI. Oral arguments transcribed and searchable.

Donor Networks

FEC filings and OpenSecrets data aggregated and visualized. Each connection verified against additional sources.

Updates

Whenever elections happen, lawsuits are filed, and redistricting news breaks. Always accurate.

Built With

Claude AI for summaries and data processing. Netlify for hosting. Open-source libraries throughout.

What this means

The impact isn't about changing policy. It's about forcing the truth into the open. When you can SEE which districts are packed, MEASURE the vote gap, and TRACE the money — you can't pretend anymore.

You can show your representative the numbers. You can vote based on facts. You can tell others exactly how broken the system is.

That's the impact: truth. Democracy needs it.
